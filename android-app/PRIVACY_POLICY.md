# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** February 14, 2026

## Overview

Expense Tracker ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use the App.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

**Authentication Information:**
- When you sign in using Google authentication, we receive and store your Google account email address and basic profile information (name, profile picture) to create and maintain your account.
- This information is used to identify you and maintain your user session.

**Expense Data:**
- When you create, edit, or delete expense entries, we collect and store:
  - Expense amount
  - Expense category
  - Expense description/notes
  - Date and time of the expense
  - Attachments or receipts (if applicable)

**User Preferences:**
- Settings and preferences you configure within the App (e.g., currency, language, notification preferences)

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

**Device Information:**
- Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
- App version and build information

**Usage Information:**
- Features you use and actions you take within the App
- Timestamps and frequency of App usage
- Crash reports and error logs (if enabled)

**Location Information:**
- The App does **not** collect your precise location. Location data, if used, is only for optional features like marking expenses by location (stored locally on your device).

**Network Information:**
- IP address and network type (WiFi or mobile data)

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Account Management:** Create, maintain, and authenticate your account
- **Service Delivery:** Process and store your expense data, synchronize data across devices
- **Feature Functionality:** Enable App features and personalization
- **Communication:** Send you service-related notifications, updates, and support responses
- **Improvement:** Analyze usage patterns to improve the App's functionality, performance, and user experience
- **Security:** Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues
- **Legal Compliance:** Comply with applicable laws and respond to legal requests

---

## 3. Data Storage and Synchronization

### 3.1 Local Storage
- Expense data is stored locally on your device using encrypted local databases
- This data persists even when you're offline

### 3.2 Cloud Synchronization
- When you have an internet connection, your expense data is synchronized with our remote server (hosted on Vercel)
- The synchronization is performed securely using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS)
- We maintain a backup of your data to enable recovery and multi-device synchronization

### 3.3 Data Retention
- Your account and associated expense data are retained as long as your account is active
- If you delete your account, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption in Transit:** All data transmitted between the App and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL protocols
- **Encryption at Rest:** Sensitive data stored locally on your device is encrypted using Android Keystore
- **Server Security:** Our backend servers implement standard security practices including authentication, authorization, and access controls
- **Secure Authentication:** Google Sign-In is used for secure authentication without storing passwords

**Important Note:** While we strive to protect your information, no security system is completely impenetrable. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.
